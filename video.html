About this video

Watch the Circuit Trace trailer: a calm neon flow puzzle game for Android. See the campaign, Classic, Diodes, Portals, Arcade, Labyrinth and the new 3D Cube, plus Endless, Expert, daily challenges, the Puzzle Creator and duels with friends.

Circuit Trace is a flow puzzle: connect each pair of matching dots with a line, never let two lines cross, and fill every cell on the board. The trailer shows how each mode twists that one rule.

What you'll see

  • Circuit Trace campaign · 100 levels in 10 sectors, one new mechanic at a time
  • Classic · Pure flow puzzles from 5×5 to 10×10
  • Diodes · One-way arrows decide where a line goes
  • Portals · Lines jump across the board
  • Arcade · Stars, diodes and checkpoints together
  • Labyrinth · Shaped boards with holes and walls
  • 3D Cube · Puzzles on all six sides of a cube
  • Endless · Fresh puzzles in seven difficulties
  • Expert · A random hard level from the late campaign

Plus the Daily Challenge, Weekly Puzzles, a 60-second Time Trial, the Puzzle Creator with its Community, duels with friends, 13 themes and the FX packs. Every mode is explained on the Game Modes page.
