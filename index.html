Questions about Circuit Trace

Quick answers about the flow puzzle game. More on the support page.

Circuit Trace is a free flow puzzle game for Android. Connect each pair of matching colored dots with a line, never let lines cross, and fill the whole grid to restore the circuit. It has 500+ handcrafted puzzles across 7 game modes.
Yes. Every puzzle, mode and the Puzzle Editor are free. Ads only appear between levels, never while you're solving. Optional purchases remove ads or add cosmetic themes and visual effects; nothing gives an advantage.
