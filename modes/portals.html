The teleport mode

Portals: jump across the board

Two linked rings, one line, and suddenly the far corner of the board is right next door.

How it works

Portals come in linked pairs. When your line reaches one, lift your finger and tap the other portal to carry on from there. The line jumps, and everything in between stays free for other colours.

Every portal in this pack is part of the route, so a portal is never just decoration. If you are stuck, ask yourself which line has to go through it.

The pack starts with one portal pair per level, moves to two in sector 4 and three from sector 8. From sector 5 every cell has to be filled.

  • ✓ 100 levels in 10 sectors
  • ✓ Boards from 6×6 to 10×10
  • ✓ 4 to 10 colour pairs
  • ✓ One to three portal pairs per level

Tips for Portals

  • Think of a portal as a shortcut that also splits the board: the stretch a line skips becomes room for another colour.
  • Look for a colour whose dots are clearly cut off from each other. That one probably needs the portal.
  • Check what is around the exit before you jump. A portal that lands in a tight corner limits where the line can go next.
