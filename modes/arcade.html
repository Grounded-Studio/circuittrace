The mixed mode

Arcade: everything at once

Stars, diodes and checkpoints on the same board. Arcade builds them up one at a time, then stops holding back.

How it works

Arcade opens with plain boards, then adds a mechanic every couple of sectors. Stars come in sector 2, diodes in sector 3 and checkpoints in sector 5.

Stars are bonus pickups: route a line through one to collect it. Diodes force a line to leave in the direction of their arrow. Checkpoints are cells a line has to pass through before the pair counts as connected.

From sector 5 every cell has to be filled, and from there the mechanics stay together on the same board until the 10×10 finale.

  • ✓ 100 levels in 10 sectors
  • ✓ Boards from 6×6 to 10×10
  • ✓ 4 to 9 colour pairs
  • ✓ Stars, diodes and checkpoints combined

Tips for Arcade

  • Read the arrows first. They give you more certainty than anything else on the board.
  • Treat checkpoints as fixed points on a route, then connect the pieces between them.
  • Stars are worth planning for, but solve the board first. On a fill-every-cell level, any route you complete passes through them anyway.
