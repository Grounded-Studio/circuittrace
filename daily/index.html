How to play

  1. Drag from a colored dot to the other dot of the same color.
  2. Lines can't cross. Drawing over another line cuts it.
  3. Connect every pair to solve the puzzle. Fill every cell for a perfect solve.
  4. Drag back along a line to undo, or start again from a dot.

The clock starts when you draw your first line. Your time and your streak stay in this browser only: no account, no cookies.

About the daily puzzle

When is there a new daily puzzle?

Every day at midnight, your local time. Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle on the same date.

Is it the same puzzle as in the app?

Yes. The web puzzle is today's Daily Challenge from the Circuit Trace app, made by the same generator with the same daily seed.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and there are no tracking cookies. Your time and streak are only stored in this browser.

How do I share my result?

After you solve the puzzle, tap Share. You get a short line with the puzzle number and your time, without any spoilers.

Where can I play more puzzles?

Circuit Trace for Android has 700+ puzzles in 9 game modes, including portals, diodes, Labyrinth and the 3D Cube, plus a Puzzle Editor. It is free on Google Play.
