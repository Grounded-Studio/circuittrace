The maze mode

Labyrinth: the board is the puzzle

Rings, towers and castles instead of squares, with holes in the middle and glowing walls your lines cannot cross.

How it works

Labyrinth boards are not squares. Cells are cut out of them to make rings, towers and castles, so a line often has to go the long way round.

Walls add a second layer. A glowing wall sits between two neighbouring cells, and no line can step across it, even though the cells look like they touch.

From sector 5 every cell has to be filled. Each level is built around one intended solution, so the shape of the board is your best clue.

  • ✓ 100 levels in 10 sectors
  • ✓ Shaped boards up to 9×14
  • ✓ 3 to 9 colour pairs
  • ✓ Holes and walls on every level

Tips for Labyrinth

  • Follow the corridors. A one-cell-wide passage can only be used by one line, so work out which colour it belongs to.
  • Dead ends need a dot. If a pocket has only one way in and no dot, the board needs a different plan.
  • Check for walls before you commit. They are easy to miss when you are dragging quickly.
