How to play

  1. Drag from a colored dot to the other dot of the same color.
  2. Lines can't cross or overlap.
  3. Diodes (arrows): a line that runs through one must leave in the arrow's direction. A colored diode only lets that color through.
  4. Portals (rings): a line that reaches one continues from its twin. Tap the other ring to jump.
  5. Checkpoints (diamonds): a colored one must be on that color's line; a white one must be covered by any line.
  6. Stars are bonus pickups.
  7. Connect every pair to solve it. Fill every cell for a perfect solve.

About puzzle codes

What is a Circuit Trace puzzle code?

A short code that starts with CT- and describes one puzzle: the grid size, the colored dots and any diodes, portals, stars or checkpoints. You get one when you tap SHARE in the Puzzle Editor of the app.

Is my data in the code?

No. A code only describes the board. It contains no name, no account and no player data. This page decodes it in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

How do I make my own puzzle?

Install Circuit Trace, open the Creator from the main menu and tap Make a Puzzle. When the solver says it is valid, tap SHARE to get your code.

How do I play a code in the app?

Open the Creator in Circuit Trace, tap Play Shared Code and paste the code. The app also offers to paste it straight from your clipboard.
