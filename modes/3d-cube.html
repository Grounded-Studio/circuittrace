3D Cube: six sides, one puzzle

The puzzle wraps around a cube. Lines run over the edges onto the next side, and the way out of one face is often on another.

How it works

Every side of the cube is a small grid, and the sides connect. Draw a line to the edge of a face and it carries straight on to the next one.

Swipe an empty spot to turn the cube. You can also drag a line off the edge towards a side you cannot see: the cube rolls that side into view and the line goes with it, in one motion. The first level walks you through both.

Every cell has to be filled to solve a level. A cube has no board edge, so there is no corner to start from. Each level is built around one intended solution.

  • ✓ 100 levels in 10 sectors
  • ✓ 3×3 and 4×4 cells on every side
  • ✓ 54 or 96 cells per puzzle
  • ✓ 6 to 12 colour pairs

Tips for 3D Cube

  • Turn the cube before you start and look at all six sides. A dot on the back often changes your first move.
  • Fewer lines means longer lines. In the later sectors expect a single colour to wrap around several sides.
  • At each corner of the cube three cells meet. Those spots are tight, so decide early which lines go through them.
