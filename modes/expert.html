The preview mode

Try Expert: skip ahead for a round

Want to know how hard this game gets? One tap drops you into a random level from deep in the campaign.

How it works

Try Expert picks a random level from sectors 5 and 6 of the campaign, so you can see what is waiting long before you get there. You will meet diodes, checkpoints and portals, and the level only counts as solved when every cell is filled.

It does not change your campaign progress, so there is nothing to lose. Try it, get stuck, come back later.

The tile is there for newer players. Once you reach sector 5 of the campaign you are playing these levels anyway, and it disappears from the menu.

  • ✓ A random hard level each time
  • ✓ Taken from campaign sectors 5 and 6
  • ✓ 7×7 boards, 6 to 7 colour pairs
  • ✓ Every cell must be filled

Tips for Try Expert

  • Connecting all the dots is only half of it. Look for empty cells early, because each one has to be part of a line.
  • Not sure what a symbol means? The Game Guide in the main menu explains every element.
  • Too hard right now? Open the tile again for a different level, or play the campaign and come back.
