How it works

Sign in to Google Play Games the first time you boot Circuit Trace (or skip and play anonymously — the game works fully offline). Once signed in:

  • ✓ Badges unlock automatically as you hit each milestone
  • ✓ Leaderboard scores push every time you set a new record
  • ✓ Progress survives new phones — just sign in on the new device
  • ✓ Zero email, phone number, or personal info required beyond your Play Games display name

Google Play Games is optional. Everything in the game works without it — you just won't see the badges or climb the global boards.
