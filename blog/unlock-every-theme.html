Guide

13 themes, and how to unlock every one.

📅 28 October 2026 · ⏱ 6 min read · ✍️ Grounded Studio

You spend a lot of time looking at a puzzle game. Hundreds of boards, the same grid, the same glowing dots. So early on we decided the background should be yours to pick.

Circuit Trace now has 13 themes. Four are free from the moment you install. Six you earn by playing. Three are exclusive to the Theme Pack. Here's the full list, what each one feels like, and how to get it.

First: how to change your theme

From the main menu, tap PROFILE. Scroll to the THEME card and tap SELECT THEME. A grid of every theme opens. Tap one and the whole game changes on the spot: menus, boards, completion cards, everything.

One thing never changes: the colours of the dots and paths. Red is always red, blue is always blue. A theme changes the mood around the puzzle, never the puzzle itself, so you never lose clarity mid-solve.

The free four

These ship unlocked for everyone. They're the "pick your mood" set.

Midnight is the original look and what most screenshots show. Amethyst is made for late-night sessions. Emerald is the calmest of the four. Sunset is warm and cosy.

The six you earn with stars

Some boards have ★ star cells on them. A star is collected when one of your paths runs through it. Every star you collect adds to your lifetime total, which you can see on your Profile. Hit a milestone and the next theme unlocks.

Volcano arrives quickly and is a nice first reward. Mono at 500 stars is the long-term goal: a clean silver look for players who have seen it all.

💡 Tip: where to find stars. Plenty of campaign levels have stars on them, and the Arcade pack is built around them: 100 puzzles that mix stars, diodes and checkpoints. If you're chasing Mono, Arcade is where you'll get there fastest.

The three exclusives: the Theme Pack

Three themes can't be earned with stars. They're the richest backgrounds in the game, each with its own colour story from top to bottom.

The Theme Pack unlocks all three for €1.99, once. There's no subscription, and it works with every mode and every FX pack. Find it in the 🛍 SHOP or at the top of the theme picker.

Not sure yet? Try one for a day

Tap a locked exclusive theme in the picker and you can watch one short video to use it for 24 hours. It's a real trial: the whole game switches over, and the tile shows how many hours you have left. When the time is up you go back to your previous theme automatically. If you love it, the pack is one tap away. If not, nothing changes.

Cosmetic, always

One promise we made at the start and kept: themes never touch gameplay. No theme makes a puzzle easier, no paid theme gives you hints, and every one of the 500 puzzles plays exactly the same in Midnight as in Cosmic Nebula. You're paying for a look you enjoy, never for an advantage.

Want the board itself to glow as well? That's what the FX packs are for. We wrote a separate post about them: Neon Circuit & Hyper Grid, a closer look.

See every theme side by side on the Themes & FX page, or install Circuit Trace and start collecting stars.
