The best logic puzzle games for Android in 2026.

📅 16 November 2026 · ⏱ 6 min read · ✍️ Grounded Studio

A good logic puzzle game gives you a problem that looks impossible, a few simple rules, and the feeling that the answer was there all along. Here are games we enjoy on Android, sorted by the kind of thinking they ask for.

Full disclosure: we make Circuit Trace, so it's on this list. Read our entry with that in mind. Everything else is here because we think it's worth your time, not because anyone asked us to include it. We don't list prices or ratings because they change; check each store page.

What makes a puzzle game worth installing

  • Fair puzzles. You should be able to solve every level by thinking, not by luck.
  • A real difficulty curve. Easy at first, then properly hard.
  • Respect for your time. Short sessions, offline play, and no ads in the middle of a puzzle.

1. Circuit Trace: flow puzzles with extra rules

Our own game. You connect matching coloured dots with lines that can't cross, on boards from 5×5 to 10×10. It has 700+ puzzles in 7 packs, and each pack adds one twist: one-way diodes, portals, shaped boards with walls, and a 3D cube where lines run around the edges. There's also a daily puzzle, a puzzle editor with share codes, and duels with friends. Every level is rated by a solver that reasons like a person. You can try it without installing: play the daily puzzle in your browser.

2. Flow Free: the classic

Flow Free, by Big Duck Games, is the game that made connect-the-dots line puzzles popular on phones. Huge level packs and a clean, simple design. If you like pure flow puzzles without extra mechanics, it's the obvious place to start. Circuit Trace's Classic pack is our take on the same pure style.

3. A good sudoku app

Sudoku is the logic puzzle most people already know: fill a 9×9 grid so every row, column and box has the digits 1 to 9. There are many sudoku apps on Android. Look for one with hand-checked puzzles, pencil marks and no time pressure.

4. Nonograms (picross)

Number clues at the edge of a grid tell you how many cells in each row and column are filled. Solve it and a picture appears. Nonograms train the same "what can't be true" thinking as hard flow puzzles. Many apps carry them under names like picross or griddlers.

5. Simon Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection

A free, open-source collection of around forty small logic puzzles, from bridges and loops to mines and towers, with an Android version available. No frills, endless puzzles, and a great way to discover which puzzle types you like.

6. KAMI 2: colour-flood puzzles

KAMI 2, by State of Play, asks you to turn a whole paper pattern into one colour in as few moves as possible. It feels calm and looks beautiful, but the later puzzles need real planning.

7. Baba Is You: change the rules

In Baba Is You, by Hempuli, the rules of each level are blocks of words you can push around. Make "wall is win" and walk into a wall. It's one of the most inventive puzzle games of the last decade, and it's on Android.

8. Monument Valley: puzzles you look at

Monument Valley, by ustwo games, is less about logic grids and more about impossible architecture: turn the world and paths connect that shouldn't. Short, gorgeous, and a nice break from number puzzles.

Which one should you pick?

  • Want quick, satisfying puzzles in five-minute sessions? Try a flow puzzle game.
  • Like numbers and deduction? Sudoku or nonograms.
  • Want variety? Simon Tatham's collection.
  • Want something that surprises you? Baba Is You or Monument Valley.

Frequently asked questions

What is a logic puzzle game?

A game where every puzzle can be solved by reasoning from a few simple rules, without luck or reflexes. Sudoku, nonograms and flow puzzles are classic examples.

Are there logic puzzle games that work offline on Android?

Yes. Most puzzle games, including Circuit Trace, work offline. Online features such as leaderboards or sharing need a connection.

Is Circuit Trace like Flow Free?

The core rule is the same: connect matching dots without crossing lines. Circuit Trace adds packs with diodes, portals, walls and a 3D cube, a daily puzzle and a puzzle editor.
