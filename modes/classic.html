The calm mode

Classic: pure flow puzzles

Coloured dots, one finger, no extra rules. Classic is the original idea and nothing else.

How it works

Connect every pair of matching dots with a line. Lines cannot cross, and each cell holds only one line. That is the whole rulebook.

In Classic you only have to connect the pairs to solve a level. Filling every cell is the bonus that earns the third star, so you can play it relaxed or go for the perfect board.

If you are new to flow puzzles, this is the best place to start after the first campaign levels.

  • ✓ 100 levels in 10 sectors
  • ✓ Boards from 5×5 to 10×10
  • ✓ 4 to 9 colour pairs
  • ✓ No arrows, portals or walls

Tips for Classic

  • Lines along the edge are rarely wrong. Hugging the border leaves the middle open for the others.
  • Going for three stars? Treat every empty pocket as a warning: a line has to pass through it somehow.
  • If two lines keep blocking each other, try drawing the other one first.
