The main campaign

The Circuit Trace campaign

Ten sectors, 100 levels, and one new idea at a time. This is where the game teaches you everything it can do.

How it works

The campaign starts with the plain rules: connect each pair of matching dots with a line, and never let two lines cross. The first three sectors use nothing else, so you can get a feel for planning routes around each other.

From sector 4 the extras arrive one by one. Stars and checkpoints come first, diodes follow, and portals appear in sector 6. From sector 5 onward the rule gets stricter too: a level only counts as solved when every cell on the board is filled.

Every solved level earns up to three stars: one for connecting all pairs, two for doing it in the fewest moves, and three for filling every cell.

  • ✓ 100 levels in 10 sectors
  • ✓ Boards from 5×5 to 9×9
  • ✓ 3 to 8 colour pairs
  • ✓ Stars, checkpoints, diodes and portals

Tips for the campaign

  • Start with the pair that has the fewest ways to go. Dots in corners and along the edge are usually the best first move.
  • When a new mechanic appears, read the short message at the top of the level. It tells you exactly what changed.
  • From sector 5, look for cells that only one line can still reach. Those decide most of the board.
